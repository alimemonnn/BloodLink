3. Health-related data

Your blood group and donation dates relate to health. Donors give separate, explicit consent for this when registering, and can withdraw it by deleting their account at any time. A donor’s own statement about availability is not a medical clearance: only a hospital decides whether someone may donate.

4. Who can see what

  • The public sees only totals and open requests showing blood group, hospital, city, progress and the patient’s initials. Never names, phone numbers or notes.
  • Logged-in patients, hospitals and admins can search the donor list. They see a donor’s name, city and blood group. A donor’s phone and email appear only if that donor ticked “share my contact details”.
  • When a donor offers to help with a request, they agree (tick box) to share their name and phone with that request’s hospital and patient.
  • The hospital named on a request sees that request’s patient and donor details. Other hospitals cannot.
  • Donors never see a patient’s name, phone or notes.
  • Administrators can see account and request data in order to run and protect the service.

5. Third parties

  • No analytics, advertising, social-media or tracking services. Fonts, icons and scripts are served from this site, so your browser does not contact other companies just by loading a page. See licences.
  • Hosting and database providers store the data on our behalf. They may be located outside Pakistan.
  • Email is sent only if the operator has configured an email service and you opted in to email alerts. Each email contains a one-click unsubscribe link.
  • Call and WhatsApp buttons appear only for donors who agreed to share their contact details. Tapping WhatsApp opens WhatsApp, which then handles that conversation under its own policy.
  • We do not sell personal data.

6. Our basis for using your data

Your consent (account registration, health data, contact sharing, email alerts), and what is necessary to provide the service you asked for. We also keep basic security logs to prevent abuse. We follow widely recognised data-protection principles: lawfulness, purpose limitation, data minimisation, accuracy, storage limitation, security and accountability, and we will comply with any data-protection law that applies to this service.

8. Your rights and how to use them

  • Access / copy: logged-in users can download their data (JSON). Others can use the data request form.
  • Correct: edit your profile, or use the form.
  • Delete: donors and patients can delete their account themselves from the profile page. Anyone else can use the deletion request form. Deleting is as easy as registering.
  • Withdraw consent (contact sharing, email alerts) in your donor profile, or unsubscribe from any email in one click.
  • Complain: you may contact the data-protection or consumer authority in your country.

For requests made without logging in, an administrator confirms your identity by email before changing or deleting anything. We aim to respond within 30 days.

9. Children

You must be {{ min_age }} or older to create a donor or patient account, and we do not knowingly collect data directly from children. A parent or legal guardian may post a request for a child; they must confirm this on the request form. If you believe a child has registered, tell us and we will remove the account.

10. Security

Passwords are hashed. Forms are protected against cross-site request forgery. Login attempts are rate-limited. Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies in production. No online service is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you we will tell you and the relevant authority where required.

11. Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the version and date above, and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.

We want everyone, including people using screen readers, keyboards or magnification, to be able to use BloodLink. We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA.

What we do

  • A “Skip to main content” link and a visible focus outline on every control; the whole site works with a keyboard.
  • Text and interface colours are checked against the 4.5:1 and 3:1 contrast ratios by an automated test.
  • Icons that only decorate are hidden from screen readers; icon-only buttons have text labels. The site has no photographs; the logo is an original vector drawing.
  • Charts have a text alternative and a data table.
  • Progress bars announce “x of y units confirmed”.
  • No auto-scrolling or flashing content; animations stop when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • Status and error messages are announced to screen readers and do not disappear on their own.

Known limits

This is a student project and has not been audited by an external accessibility specialist. If you meet a barrier, tell us and we will try to fix it.

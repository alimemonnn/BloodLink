BloodLink is free. There is nothing to buy, no subscription, no registration fee, no priority fee, no booking fee and no hidden charge, so there is nothing to refund.

What this means in practice

  • Creating an account, posting a request, searching donors, responding to a request and using the dashboards cost nothing.
  • We do not take payments, store card details, or accept tips or donations through this site.
  • Blood is never sold on BloodLink. Donors are volunteers and must not be paid. Nobody may charge for a place in a queue.

If someone asks you to pay

Anyone asking for money “through BloodLink”, for example to “confirm” a donor, “unlock” contact details or “speed up” a request, is not acting for us. Do not pay. Tell us and we will investigate and may deactivate the account.

Charges made by hospitals or blood banks

A hospital or blood bank may charge its own screening, testing or processing fees under its own rules. Those are outside BloodLink and we cannot refund them. Please ask the hospital for its refund policy before you pay.

If we ever introduce a fee

We would first update this policy and the Terms, show the full price before you commit, and not apply it to anything you had already used. We will not add charges that were not shown up front.

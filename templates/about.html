About BloodLink

BloodLink is an emergency blood donation network that links four groups of people: patients who need blood, hospitals that can confirm the need is real, volunteer donors, and the administrators who keep the platform trustworthy.

In an emergency, families lose hours calling around for a matching donor. BloodLink replaces that search with a verified request: the patient posts what is needed, the hospital confirms it, and the system finds every registered donor whose blood group is compatible and who is available in the same city.

Why hospital verification matters

Open donor directories fill up with fake or duplicated requests. In BloodLink a request stays private until the named hospital approves it, so donors only ever see needs a medical institution has confirmed.

Built as a university project

BloodLink is a Software Engineering project built with Python Flask, SQLAlchemy and SQLite, with a Bootstrap 5 front end. It uses no paid services, runs locally, and stores every account, request and response in a real database.

What each role can do
  • Donor — keep a profile, switch availability on and off, see matching requests, and respond with "I can donate".
  • Patient — post a blood request, track its status, and see which donors responded.
  • Hospital — review requests made against it, approve or reject them, add verification notes, and close them once blood is arranged.
  • Admin — manage every account, monitor requests, and read platform analytics.
Blood compatibility

BloodLink matches on real transfusion rules. O− can give to every group, AB+ can receive from every group, and a request for B+ blood can be met by B+, B−, O+ or O− donors.

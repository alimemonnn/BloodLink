How BloodLink works

From registration to a completed donation, here is the full path a request takes.

1
Register

Donors give their blood group, city and (optionally) last donation date, and agree to how their data is used. Patients add contact details. Hospitals register and wait for admin verification.

2
Post a request

A patient names the hospital, blood group, units needed, urgency and a contact number. The request starts as "Pending Hospital Approval".

3
Hospital verifies

The hospital sees the request in its dashboard and approves or rejects it, adding verification notes where useful.

4
Donors offer, hospital confirms

Compatible, available donors are notified and can offer to help. The hospital screens them and confirms each donation. The request is fulfilled only when all required units are confirmed.

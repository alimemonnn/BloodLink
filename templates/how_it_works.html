How BloodLink works

From registration to a completed donation, here is the full path a request takes.

1
Register

Donors add their blood group, city and last donation date. Patients add contact details. Hospitals register and wait for admin verification.

2
Post a request

A patient names the hospital, blood group, units needed, urgency and a contact number. The request starts as "Pending Hospital Approval".

3
Hospital verifies

The hospital sees the request in its dashboard and approves or rejects it, adding verification notes where useful.

4
Donors connect

Compatible, available donors are notified and can respond. Once blood is arranged, the request is marked fulfilled.

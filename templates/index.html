How BloodLink works

Four steps stand between a patient and a donor who can help.

1

Register

Sign up as a donor, a patient or a hospital. Donors add their blood group, city and last donation date.

2

Post or find blood

Patients post a request with the hospital, blood group, units and urgency, or search donors directly.

3

Hospital verifies

The hospital reviews the request. Only requests a hospital approves reach donors.

4

Donors offer, hospital confirms

Compatible donors nearby are notified and can offer to help. The hospital screens them and confirms each donation. A request is fulfilled when all required units are confirmed.
